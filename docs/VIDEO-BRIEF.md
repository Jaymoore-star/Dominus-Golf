## 4. Style

- **Tone:** premium and confident. Short, punchy lines. Calm authority, not
  hype. Think a high-end athletic brand.
- **Motion:**
  - Slow push-ins on photos.
  - Hard cuts on the beat.
  - Headline words revealed one at a time.
  - Thin gold lines that trace a swing arc, used as the visual motif for
    "path" and "plane".
- **Music:** royalty-free, dark, minimal, building percussion. One clear hit
  on the product reveal.
- **Captions:** burned in. Most people watch muted.
- **Narration:** none required. All text is on screen. An optional voiceover
  script is included for Video 1.

---

## 5. Rules: what the videos must NOT say or show

- **No superlatives or numbers** such as "#1", "10X", "guaranteed" or
  "proven". Use only the approved words in section 1.
- **No customer testimonials, quotes or star ratings** in ads.
- **No pro endorsements** unless the owner confirms in writing that the pro
  has approved appearing in an ad.
- No realistic AI-generated people or AI video. Use only the real photos and
  product images listed above.
- Don't show the women's or junior trainer as available (both sold out).
- Don't describe the hinge as doing something specific (for example
  "breaks if your path is wrong") unless the owner confirms that's how it
  works. Safe wording: "immediate feedback on every rep".
- The 10% offer is "join the list", never a printed code.
